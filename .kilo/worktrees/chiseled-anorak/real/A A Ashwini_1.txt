## POC: 1

**Title: Intelligent Document Question Answering using RAG Details:**

* Objective of the project is to ask questions (that are not included in the training data) about the document without finetuning the LLM and get the relevant answers
* In this POC, I have used Langchain Framework and Chroma DB as vector database and llama-3-70B-Instruct model by meta.
* All the coding and developmental activities are done with python programming integrated with VS Code, ensuring

modularity and ease of experimentation.

* Approach:
  + Questions are processed through a retrieval step to fetch relevant documents from the vector store.
  + Retrieved context is passed to the LLM to generate precise answers, even for queries not seen during training.
  + No model fine-tuning was performed, demonstrating the power of RAG in zero-shot scenarios.
* Outcome:

Successfully demonstrated the feasibility of using RAG with state-of-the-art LLMs for intelligent document Q&A, paving the way for scalable enterprise applications in knowledge management and automation.
